# Boundary Cases Corpus

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Initial Research Corpus

## Purpose

The corpus exists to test candidate invariants and prevent AltisSports from defining Spatial Sport through convenient examples alone.

Each case must be evaluated without forcing a desired conclusion.

## Evaluation Fields

Each case should eventually record:

- case identifier;
- activity;
- recognized status;
- physical agency;
- cognitive agency;
- equipment mediation;
- computational mediation;
- rule location;
- measurement method;
- comparability conditions;
- outcome structure;
- consequence structure;
- spatial integration;
- disputed questions;
- evidence sources;
- provisional classification;
- confidence;
- revision history.

## Initial Test Questions

For each case:

1. What constitutes the performing agent?
2. Which human skill materially changes the result?
3. Where do the rules reside?
4. How are rules enforced?
5. What must be calibrated?
6. What makes two performances comparable?
7. Is the result open to performance?
8. What meaningful consequence exists?
9. Does physical or computational space constrain performance?
10. Is the activity sport, sport-like, training, simulation, game, spectacle, or another class?
11. Which conclusion is disputed, and why?
12. What evidence would change the classification?

## Corpus Rule

A difficult case must not be removed because it destabilizes the theory. Destabilization is evidence.
