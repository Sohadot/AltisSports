## Synthetic Fixture Boundary

The profiles are case-derived synthetic fixtures.

They do not:

- describe a real vendor deployment;
- establish product conformance;
- represent market evidence;
- imply endorsement;
- prove safety or efficacy.

## Review Load

A reviewer receives:

- the core brief and guide;
- assigned domains and clauses;
- one to three trial profiles;
- eight core questions;
- five role-specific questions.

The package avoids requiring every reviewer to review all material.
