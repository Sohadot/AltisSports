What the evidence is, and what it is not

AltisSports begins with boundary evidence. The Atlas exposes what the governed corpus knows. It does not manufacture what the corpus does not know.

What evidence means here

Evidence in AltisSports is not a score, a ranking, a maturity verdict, or a claim that a system is or is not a sport. It is attributed, claim-classed, inspectable material that can be corrected.

Provider claims remain distinguishable from Altis interpretation. Uncertainty remains visible. Provisional theory remains provisional. A source must support the sentence for which it is cited. AI output is not treated as evidence.

Licensed corpus and Atlas material is available under CC BY 4.0. Licensing grants reuse rights. It is not publication of ASR-001, ratification, certification, or citation elevation. ASR-001 remains an unpublished Working Draft.

Descriptive Atlas

The first Altis Atlas layer is deliberately descriptive. v0.1 contains 20 records derived 1:1 from the corpus. Atlas record identifiers such as ATL-D-001 are stable derived handles. Canonical citation identity remains the boundary-case identifier (BC-NNN).

A descriptive record can state what a system is, what a provider claims, what the evidence supports, which dimensions are present, what remains unknown or unresolved, and which source object governs the record.

It does not currently issue rankings, quality judgments, maturity scores, product recommendations, certification, or conformance verdicts.

Atlas v0.1 JSON · Static distribution

Query / Reference Surface

The query surface retrieves, resolves, and filters what the Atlas already states. It does not infer, score, rank, recommend, or add anything the Atlas does not contain. Results are descriptive records, never verdicts.

Two consumption modes exist: a deterministic CLI, and a static atlas/ distribution of one JSON file per record plus resolver indexes. No server is required.

The surface will not produce similarity rankings, “closest sports,” grades, maturity levels, or derived taxonomies that are not in the Atlas.
