Claim discipline · independence · correction

A reference must be able to be wrong in public.

Authority is not announced before the work. It accumulates through work that others can quote, verify, and correct.

Authority chain

This website is a public surface. GitHub is the canonical working reference. Zenodo v0.1 is the frozen citable publication of the conceptual and architectural foundation. Explanatory writing, including on Substack, is commentary and not a source of truth.

Claim classes

Every material claim is expected to make its epistemic status visible. Source: Source and Claim Policy.

C1

Directly Documented Fact — explicitly supported by a primary source or formal record.

C2

Multi-Source Factual Synthesis — a conclusion supported jointly by credible sources.

C3

Attributed External Claim — a provider or institution claim not independently verified.

C4

Altis Analytical Interpretation — a reasoned inference from identified evidence.

C5

Provisional Category Proposition — a hypothesis, candidate invariant, or draft architectural claim.

C6

Commercial or Forecast Claim — market, adoption, performance, superiority, or future claims requiring attribution and restraint.

Provenance

Provider claims must remain distinguishable from Altis interpretation. Historical material must not silently become current. A source must support the sentence for which it is cited. AI output is not evidence.

Externally citable objects require a stable identifier whose meaning cannot silently change; an explicit claim class; inspectable attribution; an explicit license; a temporal status; a falsifiability or correction path; and preservation of superseded identity rather than identifier reuse.

Falsifiability

The Category Thesis must be revised if later evidence shows that its distinctions fail. AS³ must be revised if its layers cannot be distinguished operationally, cannot be tied to observable evidence, or encourage misleading scoring. Individual records must expose what evidence would change their interpretation.

A reference that cannot be corrected cannot become a reference.

Correction

A correction must record the affected claim, corrected wording, reason, supporting evidence, date, and downstream effects. Substantive corrections must not be silently overwritten. Decisions are append-only unless formally superseded.

Independence

AltisSports is intended to remain independent at the level that matters most: the evidence itself. Payment, sponsorship, membership, licensing, or commercial relationships may not alter evidence thresholds, claim classifications, limitations, methodology, correction rights, or conclusions. Reference authority cannot be purchased.

ASR-001 status

ASR-001 — Spatial Athletic System Evidence Profile is an unpublished Working Draft. Its purpose is evidence inspectability for one bounded, versioned system configuration. It is not a mechanism for declaring that an underlying system is a sport, safe, clinically effective, certified, superior, federation-recognized, or adopted by industry. CITE-HOLD-ASR-001 remains open.

Publication of the conceptual and architectural foundation on Zenodo does not publish, ratify, validate, or elevate ASR-001.

What AltisSports does not claim

  • certification of products
  • conformance of underlying systems
  • sports-recognition authority
  • adopted or recognized standard status
  • a universal definition of sport
  • ranking, scoring, or a maturity ladder
  • a safety or clinical standard
  • industry adoption
  • external endorsement or validation
  • ownership of the words “Spatial Sport”
