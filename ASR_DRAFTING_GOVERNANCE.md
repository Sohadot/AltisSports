# ASR Drafting Governance

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Ratified for the Next ASR Artifact

## 1. Purpose

This document governs the next concrete artifact: the ASR-001 scope draft.

It does not authorize normative requirements.

## 2. Document States

- **Scope Draft** — defines problem, object, users, inclusions, exclusions, dependencies, and non-goals.
- **Scope Approved** — scope accepted under a separate gate.
- **Working Draft** — normative and informative clauses may be developed only after requirements-drafting authorization.
- **Public Review Draft** — released for structured external comment.
- **Release Candidate** — all blocking comments resolved or explicitly dispositioned.
- **Ratified** — formally approved and versioned.
- **Superseded** — replaced while preserved historically.

## 3. Gate Separation

No state advances automatically.

A successful research sprint does not produce a standard.  
An approved scope does not authorize certification.  
A public draft does not imply industry recognition.

## 4. Normative Language

The words **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, and **MAY** are prohibited in the Scope Draft except when quoting or describing future drafting rules.

The Scope Draft uses descriptive language only.

## 5. Change Control

Every material change must identify:

- affected section;
- evidence or issue that triggered the change;
- whether scope expanded, narrowed, or clarified;
- compatibility impact;
- decision owner;
- date and version.

Silent scope expansion is prohibited.

## 6. Issue Classes

Comments and issues should be classified as:

- scope;
- terminology;
- evidence;
- normative requirement;
- conformance;
- accessibility;
- safety;
- privacy/security;
- commercial conflict;
- editorial.

Requirements and conformance issues raised during scope drafting are recorded but not resolved as normative clauses.

## 7. External Review

Before Public Review Draft status, the project should seek review from more than one relevant operator class, such as:

- sport governance or integrity;
- XR/spatial product engineering;
- performance measurement;
- accessibility/adaptive sport;
- competition operations;
- research methodology.

External review informs AltisSports; it does not transfer editorial control.

## 8. Conformance Boundary

ASR-001 may eventually define conformance only for the document or profile it specifies.

It must not imply:

- certification of a sport;
- recognition by a federation;
- product quality endorsement;
- safety certification;
- clinical efficacy;
- market ranking;
- ownership of the category term.

## 9. Commercial Independence

Commercial participation may support implementation, licensing, training, or data services. It may not buy favorable wording, reduced evidence obligations, or concealed limitations.
