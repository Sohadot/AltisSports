# Source and Claim Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Ratified Operating Policy

## 1. Purpose

AltisSports must distinguish documented fact, sourced interpretation, provisional theory, and commercial claim.

AI output is not evidence. Reference authority depends on traceability, claim restraint, correction, and versioned reasoning.

## 2. Claim Classes

- **C1 — Directly Documented Fact:** explicitly supported by a primary source or formal record.
- **C2 — Multi-Source Factual Synthesis:** a conclusion supported jointly by credible sources.
- **C3 — Attributed External Claim:** a provider or institution claim not independently verified.
- **C4 — Altis Analytical Interpretation:** a reasoned inference from identified evidence.
- **C5 — Provisional Category Proposition:** a hypothesis, candidate invariant, or draft architectural claim.
- **C6 — Commercial or Forecast Claim:** market, adoption, performance, superiority, or future claims requiring attribution and restraint.

## 3. Source Priority

1. laws, regulations, and official records;
2. standards and formal specifications;
3. peer-reviewed research;
4. first-party technical documentation;
5. official company announcements and filings;
6. reputable research with disclosed methodology;
7. high-quality professional reporting;
8. secondary commentary used cautiously.

Social posts, promotional summaries, and generated content are discovery aids, not sufficient final evidence for important claims.

## 4. Primary-Source Rule

Product capability, compatibility, launch status, price, technical requirements, and partnerships should use current first-party documentation where available.

Effectiveness, safety, training transfer, physiological benefit, or comparative superiority must not rely solely on vendor marketing.

## 5. Evidence Record

Each material record should retain:

- source title and publisher;
- canonical URL;
- publication or update date;
- access date;
- source type;
- claim class;
- supported fields;
- limitations;
- temporal status;
- revision identifier.

## 6. Temporal Status

Use explicit status such as current, announced, pilot, beta, discontinued, superseded, unverified current status, or historical.

A recent access date does not make old information current.

## 7. Prohibited Practices

AltisSports must not:

- convert a vendor claim into independent fact;
- describe a pilot as broad adoption;
- describe an announcement as deployment;
- infer effectiveness from novelty;
- infer comparability from identical metric names;
- use “standard,” “certified,” “validated,” or “compliant” without a defined basis;
- publish fabricated precision;
- hide uncertainty;
- cite a source that does not support the sentence.

## 8. AI-Assisted Research

AI may assist discovery, extraction, normalization, comparison, contradiction detection, and drafting. Every published factual claim must trace to a human-inspectable source.

## 9. Correction Policy

A correction must record the affected claim, corrected wording, reason, supporting evidence, date, and downstream effects. Substantive corrections must not be silently overwritten.

## 10. Commercial Independence

Payment, sponsorship, membership, profile maintenance, or licensing may not alter evidence thresholds, limitations, correction rights, methodology, or conclusions.

## 11. Publication Test

Before publication:

1. What exactly is claimed?
2. Which claim class applies?
3. What supports it?
4. Is the source current enough?
5. Is attribution required?
6. What uncertainty remains?
7. Could analysis be mistaken for fact?
8. Would the claim survive skeptical review?
