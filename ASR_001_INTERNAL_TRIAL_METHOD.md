# ASR-001 Internal Implementation Trial Method

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Ratified for Sprint 11  
**Working Draft:** ASR-001 v0.2 candidate core

## 1. Purpose

Sprint 11 tests whether the ASR-001 Candidate Normative Core can describe materially different bounded configurations without:

- collapsing applicability into evidence sufficiency;
- inventing evidence;
- forcing a hidden score;
- turning profile validation into system certification;
- erasing uncertainty;
- rewriting the research corpus.

## 2. Trial Classes

The internal trial set covers:

1. autonomous contest execution after human design;
2. rehabilitation support with assisted human performance;
3. intentional biological control with unresolved athletic membership;
4. human–AI performance with phase-level agency handoff;
5. contest conditions altered by a participatory audience actor;
6. remote control of a physical craft without remote synchronous competition;
7. correction and supersession across two versions of the same profile.

These classes were selected because they stress distinct clause and model boundaries rather than merely repeat successful Sprint 10 fixtures.

## 3. Trial Artifact Rule

Every trial profile:

- identifies one bounded configuration;
- cites one or more BC-001–BC-020 records as internal research anchors;
- uses synthetic configuration details where the corpus does not supply operational implementation detail;
- makes no claim about a real vendor or deployed product;
- applies all thirty clause records;
- records triggered and non-triggered clauses;
- separates applicability from evidence state;
- preserves profile-only validation.

## 4. Applicability–Evidence Separation

Sprint 11 distinguishes:

### Applicability

- `applicable`
- `not_applicable`
- `unknown`
- `disputed`

### Evidence state

- `supported`
- `partial`
- `absent`
- `unknown`
- `not_evidenced`
- `not_applicable`

A clause can be applicable while its evidence state is `not_evidenced`. This is essential for remote topology, safety limitation, BCI embodiment, and causal-attribution disclosures.

## 5. Evidence Requirements

Candidate clauses use one of:

- `declaration_required`
- `supporting_evidence_or_unresolved_state`
- `lifecycle_record_required`
- `declaration_or_manifest_required`

Declarative and lifecycle clauses do not require artificial duplication of the profile's own metadata into external evidence records.

## 6. Friction Classes

- ambiguity;
- duplicate obligation;
- untestable language;
- missing field;
- applicability defect;
- evidence-burden defect;
- lifecycle defect;
- implementation-schema defect;
- exclusion leak;
- no defect.

## 7. Clause Disposition

A trial finding can produce:

- retain;
- clarify;
- narrow;
- broaden conditionally;
- merge;
- split;
- move to informative guidance;
- defer pending evidence.

No clause is changed solely for stylistic preference.

## 8. Correction Test

The rehabilitation profile is produced in two versions.

The second version:

- identifies the first profile as superseded;
- records affected clauses and fields;
- states the reason;
- cites the correction evidence record;
- preserves the first version as an immutable trial artifact.

## 9. Exit Conditions

Sprint 11 passes when:

- all trial profiles validate structurally;
- all thirty clauses are exercised across the trial set;
- at least one autonomous, support, disputed, handoff, participatory, and remote-control configuration is represented;
- correction and supersession validate;
- every friction item has a disposition;
- v0.1 artifacts remain unchanged;
- no publication, certification, score, or public conformance claim appears.

## 10. Authorization Boundary

A PASS can authorize preparation of an operator-review package.

It does not authorize:

- sending the package;
- opening Public Review;
- publishing ASR-001;
- certification;
- external conformance claims;
- industry-adoption claims.
