# ASR-001 Scope-Approval Gate

**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies to:** `ASR_001_SCOPE_DRAFT.md` and `ASR_001_SCOPE_MODEL.json`

## Gate Criteria

### G01 — Authorized subject
The scope addresses the Spatial Athletic System Evidence Profile authorized by DEC-010.

### G02 — Bounded profile subject
The scope identifies one bounded, versioned configuration and preserves object lock.

### G03 — Problem definition
The scope states a disclosure and evidence-profile problem rather than a universal sport-definition problem.

### G04 — Users and uses
The scope identifies credible institutional, technical, research, and procurement users and non-ranking use cases.

### G05 — Evidence-backed domains
Every in-scope domain has traceability to the twenty-case corpus, theory, architecture, or evidence policy.

### G06 — Explicit exclusions
The scope preserves every exclusion required by `ASR_001_SCOPE_DRAFTING_AUTHORIZATION.md`.

### G07 — Profile-only conformance boundary
Any future conformance discussion is limited to the evidence-profile document or instance and does not certify the underlying sport or system.

### G08 — Uncertainty preservation
Unknown, disputed, partial, not-applicable, and not-evidenced conditions remain representable without numerical reduction.

### G09 — No normative clauses
The scope draft contains descriptive language only and no normative requirement keywords.

### G10 — No hidden score
The scope, model, traceability map, and gate contain no weighting, total score, maturity ladder, vendor ranking, or category-certification mechanism.

### G11 — Dependencies and historical integrity
Dependencies are explicit, and the research corpus schema is not silently converted into an ASR implementation schema.

### G12 — Deferred issues
Questions belonging to requirements, conformance, safety, privacy, implementation, or external review remain registered rather than prematurely resolved.

## Decisions

- **APPROVE SCOPE — AUTHORIZE REQUIREMENTS DRAFTING ONLY**
- **CONDITIONAL APPROVAL**
- **HOLD**

Approval does not publish ASR-001, authorize certification, or imply adoption.
