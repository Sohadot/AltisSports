# ASR-001 Reviewer Guide

**Working Draft:** v0.2  
**Review channel:** Written and asynchronous after activation  
**Review purpose:** Clause and implementation quality, not endorsement

## 1. Recommended Review Sequence

1. Read `ASR_001_OPERATOR_REVIEW_BRIEF.md`.
2. Read the assigned Working Draft domains and clauses.
3. Inspect the corresponding profile-model fields.
4. Review at least one assigned synthetic trial profile.
5. Answer the core and role-specific questions.
6. Submit each material issue through `ASR_001_CLAUSE_COMMENT_TEMPLATE.md`.
7. Complete the evidence and conflict declaration.

## 2. Review Unit

The preferred review unit is one of:

- a clause identifier;
- a profile-model field;
- an applicability trigger;
- a verification method;
- a review-package artifact;
- a cross-clause interaction.

Broad comments are accepted only when they identify the affected domains or structural problem.

## 3. Strong Review Comments

A strong comment states:

- where the issue occurs;
- the operational scenario;
- why the current text fails or creates burden;
- evidence or experience supporting the concern;
- the likely consequence;
- a proposed disposition or replacement text.

## 4. Evidence Boundary

Reviewers distinguish:

- personal operational experience;
- organizational policy;
- published rule or standard;
- empirical research;
- technical specification;
- reasoned interpretation;
- hypothetical concern.

Confidential or proprietary evidence is not required. A reviewer can describe the evidence class and limitation without disclosing protected material.

## 5. Applicability Review

For conditional clauses, reviewers test:

- whether the trigger is observable;
- whether it is too broad or too narrow;
- whether unknown and disputed states remain possible;
- whether non-applicability can be distinguished from missing evidence;
- whether one configuration is generalized beyond its object lock.

## 6. Verification Review

Reviewers test whether the clause can be checked through:

- document presence;
- field presence;
- cross-reference integrity;
- evidence traceability;
- controlled vocabulary;
- conditional logic;
- temporal consistency;
- human review;
- machine validation.

A clause is defective when the stated verification cannot establish the disclosure obligation it creates.

## 7. Safety, Accessibility, and Clinical Boundary

ASR-001 records disclosures and responsibility.

Reviewers flag any text that could be interpreted as:

- safety certification;
- clinical efficacy validation;
- universal accessibility;
- medical advice;
- substitute for sport classification or regulatory approval.

## 8. Reviewer Independence

Reviewers disclose relevant interests.

A conflict does not automatically exclude a reviewer. It changes how the comment is interpreted and attributed.

Participation does not constitute:

- endorsement;
- adoption;
- partnership;
- certification;
- federation recognition;
- approval of the Spatial Sport category thesis.

## 9. Comment Attribution

The reviewer can request:

- named attribution;
- role-only attribution;
- anonymous publication of the issue;
- internal-only attribution.

Attribution choice does not change the technical disposition process.

## 10. No Public Review

The package is not a Public Review Draft.

Do not redistribute it as an adopted standard, certification framework, or open public consultation.
