**Purpose:** Make reviewer context visible without treating conflicts as automatic exclusion.

## Interest Declaration

Declare any relevant:

- employment;
- consulting or advisory work;
- research funding;
- investment or ownership;
- vendor relationship;
- federation or governing-body role;
- intellectual-property interest;
- standards-body participation;
- litigation or regulatory interest;
- public advocacy position;
- other material relationship.

For each interest, state:

- related entity or category;
- nature of the relationship;
- current or historical status;
- period;
- relevance to the reviewed clauses.

## Evidence Declaration

For evidence or experience used in comments, identify:

- evidence class;
- whether it is public;
- whether it is first-party, independent, or mixed;
- temporal status;
- known limitation;
- whether the reviewer has permission to share it.

Confidential or proprietary material is not required.

## Compensation Declaration

State whether the review is:

- uncompensated;
- compensated by AltisSports;
- compensated by another organization;
- completed as part of ordinary professional duties.

Compensation does not create favorable-review obligations.

## Non-Endorsement Confirmation

Participation does not constitute:

- endorsement;
- adoption;
- partnership;
- federation recognition;
- certification;
- approval of the Spatial Sport category thesis.

## Use Permission

The reviewer states whether AltisSports can:

- use the comment internally;
- quote the comment anonymously;
- attribute the comment by role;
- attribute the comment by name;
- publish the issue and disposition without identifying the reviewer.

## Declaration ID

Each completed declaration receives a stable identifier linked to submitted comments.
