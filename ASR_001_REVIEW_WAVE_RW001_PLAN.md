# ASR-001 Review Wave RW-001 Plan

**Wave ID:** RW-001  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Apparatus prepared — named outreach held  
**Working Draft baseline:** ASR-001 v0.2  
**Baseline manifest:** `ASR001-OPERATOR-REVIEW-BASELINE`

## 1. Mode

Limited, asynchronous, written operator review only.

Not permitted in RW-001:

- calls or interviews as a participation condition;
- open public comment;
- Public Review Draft designation;
- certification or ranking outputs;
- endorsement or adoption claims.

## 2. Package Reference

Reviewers, once named and invited under a later owner outreach decision, receive:

- `ASR_001_OPERATOR_REVIEW_PACKAGE.md`;
- Working Draft v0.2 and clause catalog v0.2;
- assigned questions from the question bank;
- assigned trial examples from the sample set;
- comment template and schema;
- evidence and conflict declaration form.

## 3. Slot Design

Seven role slots `RWS-01` … `RWS-07` cover RC-01 … RC-07.

Named persons are intentionally absent in Sprint 13.
