# ASR-001 Working Draft Architecture Gate

**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies to:** Sprint 9 artifacts

## Gate Criteria

### W01 — Approved scope preserved
The architecture remains within D01–D15 and the exclusions approved in Sprint 8.

### W02 — Clause identifiers
Every candidate has a unique stable identifier matching its domain.

### W03 — Domain coverage
Every approved domain has candidate obligations and traceability.

### W04 — Evidence-to-clause mapping
Every candidate identifies evidence references or governance dependencies.

### W05 — Applicability
Every candidate declares an applicability class and condition.

### W06 — Verification
Every candidate declares at least one verification mode.

### W07 — Rationale and uncertainty
Every candidate identifies rationale and uncertainty handling.

### W08 — Exclusion guards
Candidate clauses do not create certification, scores, rankings, or excluded outcomes.

### W09 — Implementation boundary
The research corpus schema is not silently adopted as the ASR implementation schema.

### W10 — Informative separation
Examples and guidance remain non-normative.

### W11 — No premature publication
The document remains a governed Working Draft architecture.

### W12 — Deferred issues preserved
Unresolved safety, privacy, BCI, remote-tolerance, conformance, and external-review issues remain visible.
