## 1. Purpose

This document defines how AltisSports selects a limited written-operator-review cohort for ASR-001 Working Draft v0.2 without converting selection into endorsement, adoption, certification, or Public Review.

## 2. Owner Authorization Basis

Sprint 13 proceeds under the owner's explicit instruction to continue with strict governance after Sprint 12 readiness PASS.

That instruction authorizes:

- cohort slot design;
- class-coverage confirmation;
- activation-record completion for Wave RW-001;
- written-intake channel preparation;
- issue-intake procedure confirmation.

It does **not** authorize:

- inventing named persons as reviewers;
- sending invitations;
- opening a public comment channel;
- representing any person or organization as an adopter or endorser.

## 3. Selection Principles

1. **Competence over celebrity.** Slots are matched to reviewer classes and demonstrated operational, technical, methodological, or governance experience.
2. **Coverage, not quorum.** First-wave design covers at least four required class groups. Coverage is not a vote threshold.
3. **Independence.** Conflict and interest declarations are required before comment acceptance.
4. **Non-endorsement.** Participation cannot be marketed as approval of ASR-001, AltisSports, or the category thesis.
5. **Role-first roster until named.** Sprint 13 records stable role slots. Named assignment remains an owner-controlled act after this sprint.
6. **Written-only mode.** No call or interview is required for first-wave participation.
7. **No fabricated identity.** Placeholder slots use synthetic identifiers only.

## 4. Minimum First-Wave Coverage

| Required group | Acceptable classes |
| --- | --- |
| Operational | RC-01 or RC-02 |
| Technical or measurement | RC-03 or RC-04 |
| Human-impact or methodology | RC-05 or RC-06 |
| Evidence and data governance | RC-07 |

Wave RW-001 is designed to fill **all seven classes** so that later named assignment can satisfy the minimum without redesign.

## 5. Disqualifying Conditions for a Slot Fill

A candidate must not be assigned when:

- the person or organization requires endorsement language;
- participation is conditioned on certification, ranking, or commercial preference;
- conflict disclosure is refused;
- the only available channel is a public social thread;
- the assignment would imply federation recognition or industry adoption.

## 6. Attribution Preferences Allowed

- named;
- role-only;
- anonymous;
- internal-only.

Default for unfilled slots: `pending_owner_and_assignee_preference`.

Selection criteria are ratified for Wave RW-001 preparation.
