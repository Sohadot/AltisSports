# ASR-001 Internal Implementation Trial Gate

**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies to:** Sprint 11 artifacts

## Gate Criteria

### T01 — Authorization
Internal trials are authorized by DEC-013 and the Sprint 10 gate.

### T02 — Distinct configurations
The trial set includes autonomous, support, BCI, human–AI, participatory, and remote-control configurations.

### T03 — Full clause application
Every trial contains thirty clause records.

### T04 — Applicability–evidence separation
Applicability and evidence states are represented independently.

### T05 — Autonomous agency
Agency-regime disclosure works without a live human-performance claim.

### T06 — BCI uncertainty
Intentional biological control can be disclosed without resolving athletic membership.

### T07 — Support-system integrity
Rehabilitation support remains distinct from contest and clinical-efficacy certification.

### T08 — Participatory actors
External resource or rule actors remain separate from Performance Agents.

### T09 — Remote-control distinction
Remote control of a physical craft is not silently classified as remote synchronous competition.

### T10 — Correction and supersession
A profile revision preserves both versions and validates lifecycle links.

### T11 — Evidence burden
Declarative and lifecycle clauses avoid artificial evidence duplication while evidence-integrity clauses preserve support or unresolved states.

### T12 — Deterministic validation
All profiles and trial-level checks pass locally.

### T13 — Clause changes bounded
Clause IDs and scope remain stable; changes are evidence-backed and recorded.

### T14 — Historical integrity
v0.1 artifacts and the research corpus remain unchanged.

### T15 — Prohibited outcomes absent
No publication, certification, score, rank, unsupported tolerance, final BCI determination, or industry-adoption claim exists.

## Decisions

- **PASS — AUTHORIZE OPERATOR-REVIEW PACKAGE PREPARATION ONLY**
- **CONDITIONAL PASS**
- **HOLD**

A PASS does not authorize outreach, external review, Public Review, publication, certification, or public conformance claims.
