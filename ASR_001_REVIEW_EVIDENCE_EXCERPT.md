# ASR-001 Review Evidence Excerpt

**Purpose:** Give reviewers the minimum research context for the selected trial pressures.

## BC-005 — Drone Racing

Review relevance:

- live remote human control can preserve Performance Agency;
- control mediation does not by itself create remote synchronous competition;
- fine motor and sensorimotor demands require disclosure without automatic category resolution.

Primary review areas: D03, D04, D05, D07, D10.

## BC-010 — Autonomous Robot Competition

Review relevance:

- design and engineering agency occurs before the contest window;
- autonomous execution occurs during the contest window;
- the profile needs phase-level agency without inventing causal contribution weights.

Primary review areas: D03, D06, D09, D10.

## BC-016 — Spatial Rehabilitation Support

Review relevance:

- a technically integrated spatial system can be a support configuration rather than a contest;
- measurement and accessibility remain material;
- clinical context does not establish clinical efficacy;
- correction and supersession need durable representation.

Primary review areas: D02, D08, D09, D12, D14.

## BC-018 — Intentional Biological Control

Review relevance:

- intentional biological control can support Performance Agency;
- direct signal, decoded intention, and task state are distinct;
- athletic embodiment and category membership remain unresolved;
- biological data creates privacy and evidence-governance pressure.

Primary review areas: D03, D04, D07, D12, D13.

## BC-019 — Human–AI Performance

Review relevance:

- control can change by phase;
- permitted agency modes do not prove observed causal contribution;
- version and policy conditions are essential for comparability.

Primary review areas: D03, D07, D08, D10, D14.

## BC-020 — Participatory Audience

Review relevance:

- an external actor can alter resources or constraints;
- material influence does not automatically create Performance Agency;
- rule authority and outcome integrity remain central.

Primary review areas: D06, D09, D11, D14.

## Interpretation Rule

The excerpt explains why review questions exist.

It is not a hidden weighting model, category score, or substitute for the full corpus.
