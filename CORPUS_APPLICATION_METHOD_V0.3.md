# v0.3 Corpus Application Method

**Asset:** AltisSports  
**Sprint:** Sprint 7 — v0.3 Corpus Application and Standardization-Readiness Gate  
**Status:** Ratified Research Procedure — Not a Standard  
**Schema:** `BOUNDARY_CASE_SCHEMA_V0.3.json`

## 1. Purpose

Sprint 7 tests whether schema v0.3 works beyond the cases that caused its creation.

It does so by:

1. human-reviewing the migrated BC-001–BC-010 derivative;
2. applying v0.3 relations to BC-011, BC-012, BC-013, BC-014, and BC-016;
3. preserving Sprint 6 findings for BC-015, BC-017, BC-018, BC-019, and BC-020;
4. assembling one reviewed BC-001–BC-020 research corpus;
5. deciding whether ASR scope drafting may begin.

The purpose is application and readiness assessment, not standard issuance.

## 2. Historical Integrity

The following remain unchanged:

- `boundary-cases-001-010.json`

- `boundary-cases-011-020.v0.2.json`

Sprint 7 creates new reviewed derivatives. It does not retroactively rewrite the evidence trail.

## 3. Human Review Rule

A record may be marked `human_reviewed_v0_3` only when:

- the classified object remains locked;
- the performance window is checked;
- Performance Agency is checked against the human narrative;
- Operational Spatial Integration functions remain traceable;
- `agency_segments` are used only where a phase or regime adds information;
- `distributed_arena_relation` distinguishes site topology from ordinary spatial skill;
- participatory actors are not silently converted into performers or officials;
- intentional biological control remains separate from athletic-embodiment judgment;
- uncertainty remains visible.

Human review does not turn a provisional category finding into a standard.

## 4. Sparse-Structure Rule

New fields are not page-filling requirements.

- A stable single-agency case may use one segment.
- A case with no participatory actor uses an empty relation array.
- Intentional biological control remains `null` unless the case contains the relevant control channel.
- A distributed-arena relation may be `not_applicable`, `unknown`, or `not_evidenced`.
- Unknown must not be converted into absence.

## 5. Application Cases

The v0.3 structures are deliberately applied beyond their motivating cases:

- BC-003 tests a non-performing information actor in motorsport.
- BC-005 tests remote control of a physical craft.
- BC-008 tests remote synchronous embodied competition.
- BC-010 tests pre-contest engineering followed by autonomous execution.
- BC-013 tests remote synchronous computational contest conditions.
- BC-014 tests co-located physical–computational arena alignment.
- BC-016 tests supervised support-system participation.

## 7. Prohibited Inferences

Sprint 7 does not authorize:

- category membership by field completion;
- a score or level;
- minimum embodiment thresholds;
- remote-synchronous conformance rules;
- BCI athletic classification;
- causal-attribution scoring;
- certification;
- vendor ranking;
- ASR-001 issuance.

## 8. Revision Trigger

Revise this method if applying v0.3 requires invented relations, conceals unresolved evidence, or repeatedly forces cases into fields that add no analytical value.
