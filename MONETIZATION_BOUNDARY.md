# Monetization Boundary

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Ratified Foundation Policy

## Objective

AltisSports must generate durable, respectable revenue without allowing commercial incentives to determine reference judgments.

## Open Authority Layer

The following should remain publicly accessible in stable form:

- core category definitions;
- first-principles documents;
- high-level ontology;
- methodology;
- source and claim policies;
- public standard summaries;
- version history;
- correction process;
- selected atlas records;
- machine-readable public definitions.

## Paid Intelligence Layer

Revenue may be generated from:

- professional intelligence memberships;
- expanded atlas access;
- data exports;
- change alerts;
- market and technology briefs;
- vendor and platform comparison datasets;
- institutional readiness assessments;
- research briefings;
- commercial API access;
- licensed integrations;
- commercial use of protected Altis marks, tools, and data products.

## Permitted Evidence Profile Services

A company may pay for:

- structured evidence intake;
- verification workflow;
- correction and update handling;
- expanded documentation fields;
- recurring profile maintenance;
- analytics relating to its profile.

Payment may not purchase:

- a favorable rating;
- omission of material limitations;
- fabricated verification;
- elevated editorial rank;
- suppression of competitors;
- a claim of certification where none exists.

## Prohibited Revenue Models

- paid rankings;
- undisclosed advertorials;
- thin affiliate pages;
- speculative token or NFT schemes;
- deceptive certification;
- display-ad dependence;
- automatic mass-generated directory pages;
- selling personal or sensitive athletic data;
- licensing claims beyond the evidence.

## Governing Test

A revenue mechanism is acceptable only when all answers are yes:

1. Does it preserve independent judgment?
2. Does it add structured value?
3. Is the commercial relationship visible?
4. Can the public reference layer remain credible?
5. Does it increase rather than consume the asset's long-term strategic value?
