# ASR-001 Candidate Conformance Boundary

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Non-public Working Draft boundary

## 1. Conformance Subject

Candidate conformance applies only to:

- an ASR-001 evidence-profile document; or
- a machine-readable ASR-001 profile instance.

It does not apply to the underlying sport, activity, contest, product, platform, organization, or market category.

## 2. Candidate Conformance Questions

A future profile-conformance process can ask whether:

- the profile subject is bounded and versioned;
- applicable clauses are disclosed;
- conditional applicability is recorded;
- evidence records are traceable;
- uncertainty states are preserved;
- profile versions, corrections, and supersession are visible;
- a machine-readable instance satisfies deterministic structural checks.

## 3. Explicit Non-Meanings

Profile conformance does not mean:

- the underlying system is a sport;
- the system is safe;
- a clinical claim is effective;
- a product is high quality;
- a federation recognizes the activity;
- the system belongs conclusively to Spatial Athletic Sport;
- the system is superior to another product;
- the vendor is endorsed;
- the category term is owned by AltisSports.

## 4. Candidate Result Vocabulary

Future internal trials can use:

- `profile_conforms_to_working_draft`;
- `profile_partially_conforms`;
- `profile_does_not_conform`;
- `profile_requires_human_review`;
- `profile_validation_blocked`.

These are internal Working Draft results and cannot be presented as public certification.

## 5. Certification Hold

No certification body, mark, badge, register, public claim, or commercial certification service is authorized.

## 6. Public-Review Hold

The Candidate Normative Core needs internal implementation trials, issue disposition, hosted validation, and external operator review planning before Public Review Draft status can be considered.
